frequency-words-fr.txt is derived from Lexique 3.83, a lexical database of French.

Source
------
Lexique 3.83 (Lexique383.tsv), by Boris New, Christophe Pallier, Marc Brysbaert
and Ludovic Ferrand.

lexique.org has lapsed and its domain is now parked, so the archive has to be
fetched from the Wayback Machine:

    http://web.archive.org/web/20240330115437id_/http://www.lexique.org/databases/Lexique383/Lexique383.zip

Documentation and the project's own README are mirrored at
https://github.com/chrplr/openlexicon/tree/master/datasets-info/Lexique383

Citation
--------
New, B., Pallier, C., Brysbaert, M., & Ferrand, L. (2004). Lexique 2: A New
French Lexical Database. Behavior Research Methods, Instruments, & Computers,
36(3), 516-524.

New, B., Brysbaert, M., Veronis, J., & Pallier, C. (2007). The Use of Film
Subtitles to Estimate Word Frequencies. Applied Psycholinguistics, 28(4),
661-677.
